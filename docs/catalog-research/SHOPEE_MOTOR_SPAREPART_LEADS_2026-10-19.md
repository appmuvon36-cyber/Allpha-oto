## Evidence handling rules

- These are **40 research leads**, not 40 approved catalog records. Several URLs are shop pages; product identity, exact listing URL, price, condition, and sales context must be rechecked.
- Null means the search snippet did not expose a reliable value; it must not be guessed.
- A templated string such as “Terjual/Bulan: {sold}” is invalid evidence and must be discarded.
- Sales numbers are snapshots from search snippets and may refer to the shop rather than the exact SKU. Do not rank products on these numbers until verified on the product detail page.
- Prices are observed snippets, not recommended OtoClinic prices. Compare only same SKU/part number, package quantity, condition, and warranty; then calculate low/median/P75/high.
- No image file was downloaded by this research step. Shopee listing photos are copyrighted/rights-unverified until permission or another valid rights basis is established.
- Do not publish a product until source URL, current price, condition, stock freshness, rights evidence, and applicable fitment review are resolved.
